A little privacy.

Your favorites stay here

This games launcher saves your favorite games and recently opened games in this browser on this device. No account is required. This information is not sent to Wonderworld Labs. Clearing site data removes these preferences. You can clear recently opened games from the launcher and remove favorites with their heart buttons.

Each game has its own space

Games open on their own subdomains and have their own privacy policies and local progress. The launcher does not read or synchronize your gameplay, scores, saves or model downloads across those sites.

Hosting and fonts

Cloudflare hosts this static website and may process ordinary connection and security logs. Google Fonts supplies the display fonts and receives normal font requests, including connection information such as your IP address. The launcher has no advertising or analytics scripts.
