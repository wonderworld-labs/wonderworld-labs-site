Favorites and recent games

The arcade saves your favorites and recently selected games in this browser on this device. It reopens your last selected game when you return. No account is required. These preferences are not sent to Wonderworld Labs. Remove favorites with the heart button; clearing this site's browser data removes all arcade preferences.

Games inside the arcade

The player loads each game directly from its original Wonderworld subdomain. The arcade does not host a second copy of any game. Each game keeps its own privacy policy and saves progress on its own origin. The arcade does not read or synchronize game saves. Browser privacy settings may affect whether embedded and standalone views share storage. Games you select stay loaded while you switch between them in the same visit. Reloading the arcade reloads games from their own saved progress.

Hosting and fonts

Cloudflare hosts this static website and may process ordinary connection and security logs. Google Fonts supplies the display fonts and receives normal font requests, including connection information such as your IP address. The arcade has no advertising or analytics scripts.
