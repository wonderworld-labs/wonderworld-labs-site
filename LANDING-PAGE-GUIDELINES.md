# App landing pages

Owner-selected reference: https://vaultaire.app/ (reviewed 31 October 2026). Use its clear product narrative and progressive detail as inspiration. Keep Wonderworld Labs branding, original artwork, and app-specific facts.

- Lead with the user benefit, followed by a plain description naming the platform and primary activity. Keep a visible action leading to the app's download section.
- Pair the hero with a strong product visual. Use verified screenshots when available; label illustrations honestly. Never present an invented interface as a screenshot.
- Show three concise, verified essentials, followed by short feature cards, a three-step getting-started walkthrough, a clear local-data/permission explanation, and a small set of practical FAQs.
- Use generous space, large readable headings, restrained color, contrasting panels, and consistent calls to action. Preserve mobile readability, keyboard access, visible focus, and reduced-motion support.
- Answer actual product questions: offline setup, account requirements, permissions, local storage, export, deletion, and current availability. Include limitations where they affect the user's choice.
- Never borrow another app's features, ratings, reviews, pricing, encryption claims, comparisons, or download availability. Do not invent testimonials or proof of performance.
- Keep long articles and keyword-led educational material in `/guides/`. Each app landing page must directly link every published guide for that app in a compact resources section, maintained by build_guides.py. Keep article bodies in the guide pages. Use the shared navigation, footer, palette, and typography across product, guide, category, and policy pages.
- Use unique canonical/social metadata, accurate SoftwareApplication structured data, and visible content that supports the metadata. No unsupported offers or ratings. The owner's forbidden public term remains prohibited in page text, metadata, and new URLs.
- Preserve all policy URLs and wording. Verify actual download destinations before changing availability language. The planned music web version remains planned until a working release is verified.

Daily publishing: review these guidelines before changing app pages. Improve an existing answer or article when intent overlaps; do not lengthen a landing page or add thin pages to meet a quota. Recheck layout at desktop and mobile widths after structural changes.
