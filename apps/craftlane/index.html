Craftlane explained

A few details before you create.

What can I make with Craftlane?

Use the browser studio for thumbnails, animated text, video resizing, caption formatting and hook ideas. Open the relevant tool to see its controls and export options.

Which formats can I export?

Depending on the tool, exports include PNG, WebM, SRT and VTT. Check the selected tool for the formats it produces.

Does Craftlane upload my selected media?

Selected photos, videos and text are processed on your device. Read the studio’s privacy details for its website and advertising practices.
