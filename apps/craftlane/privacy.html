THE IMPORTANT STUFF

Your files.
Your business.

Craftlane is built so your creative work happens on your device. We don't maintain accounts, store uploaded media or send your photos, videos, captions or ideas to a processing server.

Media and text

When you select a file, your browser provides temporary access to that local file. Our JavaScript processes it locally using browser APIs. Exports are downloaded to your device. Files are not transferred to Craftlane.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

Craftlane uses Google AdSense to support its free tools. When advertising is available, Google and its partners may use cookies, local storage, IP addresses, device information and page activity to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and personalise ads where permitted and consented to. Your imported photos, videos and text remain processed locally by Craftlane; they are not supplied to Google as advertising data. Learn how Google uses information from partner sites and manage personalisation at My Ad Center. Where available, use the advertising privacy message to manage or withdraw consent. Ad availability depends on Google approval and visitor choices.

Offline cache

If your browser supports service workers, the website may cache static website code so it can load offline. It does not intentionally cache your imported media or export files.

Your controls

You can clear browser site data to remove any cached site files. You can remove downloaded results through your device's own file manager. Avoid importing sensitive third-party data unless you are authorised to process it.
