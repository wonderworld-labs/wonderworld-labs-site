Know what you’re looking at.

What is Signal & Scale?

Signal & Scale is a collection of source-led visual data stories about the economy, people and technology. It pairs visual explanations with supporting references and dates.

Where can I find the sources?

Open a story and read the references and dates supplied alongside it. Use those sources to inspect the underlying evidence.

Are the charts updated in real time?

No. Stories are snapshots, and data refreshes are validated. Check the date and context of a story before using its figures.
