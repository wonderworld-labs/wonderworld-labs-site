Arrow unleashed

Offline Arrow Puzzle Game for Android

Arrow unleashed is an offline arrow puzzle game for Android. Play puzzles, return for daily challenges, and keep track of milestones on your own device without an account or an internet connection.

What you can do

Puzzles and daily challenges

Continue your puzzle progress and track daily puzzle completions. Hints and remaining lives are saved alongside your progress.

Local milestones

Follow XP, badges, completion dates, and daily goal progress as you play. Your milestones stay in private app storage on your phone.

Offline play with optional vibration

The current release does not request internet permission and includes no ads or in-app purchases. Optional vibration feedback can be turned off in settings.

Frequently asked questions

Can I play Arrow unleashed without internet?

Yes. The current Android release works offline and does not request internet permission.

Are there daily puzzles and progress tracking?

Yes. The app stores daily puzzle completions, puzzle progress, XP, badges, and daily goal progress locally.

Does the game include ads or in-app purchases?

The current release has no advertising, in-app purchases, analytics SDKs, or accounts.

How do I delete game progress?

Clear the app storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. Restarting a single puzzle does not erase all progress, settings, or milestones.
