Before you begin

A few useful answers.

Can I play without Wi-Fi?

The current Android release works offline and does not request internet permission.

Are hints unlimited?

Hints and remaining lives are saved locally. Check your installed version for the available options; unlimited hints are not promised.

Can I move progress to another phone?

There is no developer account or server copy to restore. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes local progress.
