What is Explain Studio?

Explain Studio is an Android video recording and editing app with a teleprompter, optional on-device English voice follow, and local video rendering. AI assets are made outside the app and imported.

Before you begin

A few useful answers.

Does the app generate AI assets?

AI asset creation happens outside the app. You can import prepared materials in a production pack.

Why are camera and microphone needed?

Recording uses the camera and microphone. English voice follow processes speech on the device.

Where do exports go?

You choose a destination through Android. A cloud destination follows that provider’s storage practices.

Now in your browser

Explain Studio, wherever you open a tab.

Production-pack import, sentence teleprompter, local recording and edited MP4 export. The web edition runs on your device and keeps its data in this browser. Android and browser progress are separate.
