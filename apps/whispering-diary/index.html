Before you begin

A few useful answers.

Does chat work offline?

Ordinary conversations work offline after the initial model download and setup.

Does Wonderworld Labs receive my diary?

There is no developer-operated chat server. Initial model delivery uses Hugging Face; exports use the destination you choose.

Is Elias a real person or adviser?

Elias is fictional entertainment for adults. Replies may be inaccurate and are not professional or emergency advice.
