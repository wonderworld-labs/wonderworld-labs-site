The Whispering Diary

Offline AI Character Conversations for Android

The Whispering Diary is a fictional character-conversation app for adults on Android. Talk with Elias, a character generated by a small local AI model, with ordinary conversations running on your phone after initial setup.

Download optionsRead the app privacy policy

What you can do

Local conversations after setup

The app first downloads an approximately 805 MB Qwen model from Hugging Face. After setup, ordinary conversations work without an internet connection or cloud inference.

Your conversations and memories

Conversations, drafts, preferences, and user-approved memories are stored in app-private storage. Optional diary lock uses your device biometric or screen-lock system.

Export and deletion controls

Export conversations through the Android document picker to a destination you choose. Settings provides deletion and erase controls. Response flags are saved locally and are not sent to Wonderworld Labs.

Frequently asked questions

Is The Whispering Diary fully offline?

Initial setup requires a one-time model download of approximately 805 MB from Hugging Face. After setup, ordinary conversations do not require internet.

Who is Elias?

Elias is an original fictional character generated by a small local AI model. Responses may be inaccurate. The app is entertainment for adults and does not provide professional or emergency advice.

Are diary conversations sent to a chat server?

No. Wonderworld Labs does not operate a chat server or receive diary entries. The model download does not include diary entries.

Are there ads, accounts, or subscriptions?

The current release has no account, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, subscriptions, or purchases.
