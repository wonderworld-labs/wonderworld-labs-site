Sarkarijob Search explained

Before you apply.

Is Sarkarijob Search a government website?

No. It is an independent recruitment discovery service. Government departments and recruiting organisations publish the official notices and application requirements.

How should I check eligibility and deadlines?

Open the official recruitment notification linked from the listing. Confirm qualifications, age rules, dates, fees and the application process with the recruiting organisation.

Does using the portal guarantee a job?

No. The service helps you discover notices. Eligibility decisions, selection and appointments belong to the recruiting organisation.
