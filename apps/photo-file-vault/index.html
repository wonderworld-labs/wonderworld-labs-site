What is Photo & File Vault?

Photo & File Vault keeps encrypted copies of selected photos and files in an offline Android vault. No recovery service is provided for a lost unlock secret, and exported files are unencrypted.

Before you begin

A few useful answers.

Does importing delete the original?

No. Importing creates an encrypted copy. Originals remain at their source and must be managed separately.

Can you recover a forgotten secret?

No recovery service is provided. Keep your unlock secret safe; losing it may mean losing access to the encrypted files.

Are exported files encrypted?

Exports are unencrypted. Protect the destination and any additional copies you create.
