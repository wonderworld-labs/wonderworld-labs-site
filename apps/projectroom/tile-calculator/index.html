How the estimate works

Multiply tile length plus grout gap by tile width plus grout gap for nominal coverage per tile. Divide the net project area with allowance by this coverage, then round up to tiles and to whole boxes. A 120 ft² area with 12 × 12 in tiles, zero gap and 10% extra needs 132 tiles, or 14 boxes at 10 per box.

This gives an area-based estimate. Borders, staggered patterns, large cuts, and reuse of offcuts can change the actual tile count.

Which tile is cheaper after box rounding?

For 132 ft² including waste, tile that covers 12 ft² per box needs 11 boxes. At $28 per box with $35 delivery, it costs $343. A tile that covers 15 ft² per box needs 9 boxes. At $33 per box with free delivery, it costs $297. The higher-priced box costs $46 less for this room.
