How the estimate works

Multiply each section’s area by its depth, add the volumes and apply your allowance. A 10 × 10 ft area at 3 in deep comes to 25 ft³, or about 0.926 yd³, before allowance.

Use your supplier’s loose bulk density for the weight estimate. Confirm how the supplier sells and rounds delivery quantities. The cost comparison uses your prices and excludes delivery and fees.
