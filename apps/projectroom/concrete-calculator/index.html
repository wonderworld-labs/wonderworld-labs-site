How the estimate works

Multiply each section’s area by its depth, then add their volumes and your allowance. A 10 × 10 ft slab at 4 in deep comes to about 1.235 yd³ before allowance. Use the mixed yield printed on the bag to convert the total to whole bags.

Compare bagged concrete with ready-mix delivery

Enter both prices in the same currency. Bag cost uses whole bags. The ready-mix cost uses the larger of your estimated volume and the supplier’s billed minimum, rounded up to the supplier’s order increment. It includes any delivery and applicable short-load fee you enter. Labor, equipment, and taxes are outside this estimate.

For a 0.8 yd³ order at $150 per yd³, a 1 yd³ billed minimum makes the material cost $150. With $75 delivery and a $50 fee on billed orders below 2 yd³, the ready-mix total is $275. Use project specifications for slab and footing dimensions.
