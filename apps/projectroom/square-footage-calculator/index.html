How the estimate works

A room measuring 12 ft 6 in by 10 ft 4 in covers about 129.167 ft². Add each room you plan to tile, subtract areas you will leave bare, then use the total to compare tile boxes and delivery costs.

Split an L-shaped room into non-overlapping rectangles. Add each rectangle and subtract any measured cutouts. For a triangle, use the perpendicular height. A 12 × 10 ft room minus a 2 × 3 ft cutout gives 114 ft².

Open Tile with the same measurements to turn that area into boxes. The sketch shows individual shapes and does not check how they fit together.
