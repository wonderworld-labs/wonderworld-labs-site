Before you begin

A few useful answers.

Do I need a real instrument?

On-screen practice introduces notes and patterns. A physical instrument helps you develop touch, fingering, and playing technique.

Is there a browser version?

A web version is planned. The current product is an Android app; this website does not offer a playable instrument.

What does the microphone do?

Optional practice and the guitar tuner analyze sound on your device without saving or uploading audio.
