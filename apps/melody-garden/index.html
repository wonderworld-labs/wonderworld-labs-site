Melody Garden

Offline Piano & Guitar Learning for Android

Melody Garden is an offline piano and guitar learning app for children, teens, and adults. Choose an instrument and learning age range, then build a practice routine with lessons and progress saved on your phone.

Download optionsRead the app privacy policy

What you can do

Piano and guitar practice

Explore both instruments with lessons adapted to your selected learning age range. On-screen practice lets you get started without granting microphone access.

Optional live pitch feedback

Use microphone practice or the guitar tuner when you want sound-based feedback. Sound is analyzed live on your device; microphone audio is not recorded, saved, or uploaded.

A practice routine that stays with you

Lesson progress, practice results, favorites, and instrument preferences are stored locally, so you can return to your learning without creating an account.

Frequently asked questions

Can I learn piano and guitar offline?

Yes. The current Melody Garden Android release supports offline piano and guitar learning, with progress stored on your device.

Do I need to allow the microphone?

No. You can deny microphone permission and use on-screen practice. Microphone access is optional for microphone practice and the guitar tuner.

Does Melody Garden have ads or an account?

The current release is ad-free and has no account or developer-operated server.

How can I reset lesson progress?

Use Reset progress in Settings. Uninstalling the app also removes its local app data.
