Before your first puzzle

Make the connections clearer.

What am I trying to solve?

Arrange six tiles in a pyramid so every parent-child pair satisfies the selected rule. The six connections must all work in the same layout.

Can I play without dragging?

Yes. Tiles support tap-based swapping as well as drag-and-swap interaction.

What happens when I undo a swap?

Undo restores the previous arrangement without using a puzzle-check attempt. The available undo history resets after reshuffling or using a hint.
