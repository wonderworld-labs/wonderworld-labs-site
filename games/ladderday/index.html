Before your first puzzle

A few words about the rules.

What is a word ladder?

A word ladder connects a starting word and a destination through valid words, changing one letter at each step. Ladderday uses four-letter words and a fixed destination, GOLD.

Can I practise after the daily puzzle?

Yes. Practice mode gives you another starting word. Daily progress and practice are kept separate.

Will my streak follow me to another device?

No. Progress and streaks are saved in this browser. Clearing site data or changing browsers means starting with separate records.
