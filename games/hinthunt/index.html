Before your first puzzle

A little context for your hunt.

Do I need to download anything?

The first use of semantic mode downloads roughly 23 MB of model weights plus runtime files from third-party providers. Later use depends on browser caching and device support.

Are guesses sent to an AI server?

No. Semantic inference runs on your device. jsDelivr and Hugging Face deliver the runtime and model files and may receive normal request metadata.

Is Orbit a generative chatbot?

No. Orbit combines authored clues with pretrained word-similarity scoring. The clues are hand-written, and the game does not generate open-ended chat responses.

Can I play offline?

Only when the required game, model and runtime assets remain available in the browser cache. Complete offline availability is not guaranteed.
