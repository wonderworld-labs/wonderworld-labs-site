Privacy Policy

Wonderworld Labs · Effective 27 October 2026

Scope and provider

Wonderworld Labs operates this website and an internal reporting tool for authorised account owners. The tool reads Google Search Console performance reports and Google Ads keyword planning information to help us understand how people discover our apps and improve our website content. It is not a public account registration service.

This policy covers the website, support correspondence, and these connected reporting tools. Our Android apps have separate app privacy policies; connecting a Google account to our reporting tool is not required to use those apps.

Information accessed with your permission

When an authorised owner connects a Google account, we receive authorisation tokens that permit the approved API requests. We do not receive your Google password. Search Console data can include verified site addresses, access permissions, search queries, page addresses, countries, device categories, dates, impressions, clicks, click-through rates, and average positions. Google Ads data can include accessible customer account identifiers, keyword ideas, and available historical keyword estimates.

Search Console uses read-only access. Google's Ads authorisation scope permits broader account operations, but our reporting tool uses it only to read account and keyword planning information. It does not create or modify campaigns, ads, budgets, or billing. Availability depends on the account's permissions and Google's API approval.

How we use information

We use connected data to prepare private reports, compare website performance over time, identify useful topics, and improve our own app information and guides. We do not sell Google account data, use it for personalised advertising, or publish credentials, account identifiers, or raw private reports on this website. Public articles may reflect topics identified through this work.

Wonderworld Labs' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used to train general-purpose artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

Storage, access, and sharing

Authorisation tokens and downloaded reports are kept in a private local workspace, separate from the public website repository. Access is limited to authorised operators and tools used to run and review the reporting workflow. Google processes API requests under its own policies. We may disclose information where required by applicable law. We do not provide raw connected-account data to advertisers or data brokers.

We retain tokens while a connection is needed and reports while they are needed for comparisons and operation of the tool, unless deletion is requested. No fixed automatic deletion period is currently configured. Revoking access prevents future authorised requests but does not automatically remove previously downloaded reports.

Your choices and deletion requests

You can withdraw access through your Google Account connections. To request deletion of stored tokens, reports, or support correspondence, contact contact@wonderworldlabs.com and identify the account or site concerned without sending passwords or tokens. We may ask you to confirm that you control the account before acting on a request.

Website visits and contact

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to serve and protect the site; see its privacy statement. We have not added advertising, analytics, or tracking scripts to this website. If you email us, we receive your email address and the content you choose to send, and use them to respond and handle your request.

Children, updates, and contact

The connected reporting tool is intended for authorised adult account administrators. Children's use of our Android apps is covered by the relevant app policy. We will update this page when our practices change and seek additional consent where required. Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@wonderworldlabs.com.
