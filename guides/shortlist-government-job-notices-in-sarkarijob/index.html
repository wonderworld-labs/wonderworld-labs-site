Shortlist Government Job Notices in Sarkarijob Search

By Wonderworld Labs · Published 2026-11-14

Search Sarkarijob by post name, recruiting body or advertisement number. Use the available filters to narrow the results, then record the official reference, the relevant post and the confirmed deadline for each notice you want to keep.

Start with one search and narrow it

  1. Enter the name of a role, recruiting body or an advertisement number you already know.
  2. Use the available filters to narrow the results. If you find nothing, remove one filter and try a shorter search term.
  3. Open a promising listing and use its source link to check the recruiting organization and advertisement reference.
  4. Keep a shortlist with the reference number, exact post, source link and closing date shown by the recruiting organization.

Keep the individual post visible in your shortlist

One advertisement can cover multiple posts. Record the post name alongside the advertisement number so you can return to the right section of the official document. If two listings share a reference, compare the post details and source before treating them as separate notices.

If a date is absent or unclear, mark it as unconfirmed in your shortlist. Review the issuing organization’s notice and amendments before making plans around a deadline.

Reopen the source when you are ready to apply

Confirm the current application instructions and requirements for the specific post. Keep the official notification and any correction together so you know which details changed.

Use the recruiting organization’s application instructions to take the next step. Sarkarijob Search helps you find and organize notices. The recruiting organization decides eligibility and selection.

Questions and answers

What should I search for if I know the advertisement number?

Start with the number and the recruiting organization. Compare them with the official notice linked from the listing.

What if the listing is missing a closing date?

Treat the date as unconfirmed until you find it in the official notice or an amendment from the recruiting organization.

Where do I apply?

Follow the instructions and application link provided by the recruiting organization.
