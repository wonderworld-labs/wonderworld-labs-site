Spend Bill Gates’ Money: Try a Remaining-Balance Challenge

By Wonderworld Labs · Published 2026-11-14

Pick a fictional balance to leave unspent. Use the difference between your target and the displayed balance as the amount you can allocate. Check the quantity and receipt after each purchase.

  • Use prices to choose the next quantity
  • Use the receipt to revise your selection

Set the finish line before buying

Choose a remaining balance and limit yourself to a few different items. Look through the catalog and decide which purchases will account for most of the difference.

Use prices to choose the next quantity

Divide the remaining amount you want to spend by an item’s price. Round down to a whole number of units, enter that quantity, and read the balance again. A lower-priced item may help you get closer to your target.

Use the receipt to revise your selection

Look for the purchase that contributes most to the difference between your current and target balance. Adjust that quantity or use Sell, then recalculate. Try the challenge again with a different limit on item types.

Questions and answers

How do I get closer without going past my target?

Use the displayed balance and round your calculated quantity down. Then check whether another item fits the remaining difference.
