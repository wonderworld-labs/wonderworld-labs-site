Draw a Perfect Circle: A Three-Attempt Practice Routine

By Wonderworld Labs · Published 2026-11-14

Start with a comfortable radius and draw one complete circle at your usual pace. Review the colored trace, then use two more attempts to work on the section that wandered farthest from your intended path.

  • Change the pace on the difficult arc
  • Practice returning to the start

Use the first attempt as a baseline

Choose a starting point with room around the center dot. After drawing the circle, look for the arc where the trace changes color. Notice whether it happened early in the turn, halfway around, or as you returned to the start.

Change the pace on the difficult arc

Use the same starting point and approximate radius. Ease your pace slightly as you approach the problem area, then continue in one motion. Compare the same part of the two traces.

Practice returning to the start

On your third attempt, look ahead to your starting point before you reach the final quarter. Let the curve bring the line back around. Compare the closing arc and your score with your first attempt.

Questions and answers

Should I change the circle size every time?

Keep it about the same during this routine so you can compare how the pace and closing arc affect the result.
