Piano app not detecting notes? Microphone checks for Melody Garden

By Wonderworld Labs · Published 2026-10-27

If Melody Garden microphone practice is not responding, check that you selected microphone practice, allowed microphone access, and are playing a clear sound in a quiet space. If you prefer not to use the microphone, you can deny permission and continue with on-screen practice. Clearing lesson progress is not a first troubleshooting step.

First, separate microphone input from on-screen practice

Melody Garden has on-screen practice as well as optional microphone practice and a guitar tuner. A touch exercise does not require microphone permission. Before changing phone settings, check which activity you are using and whether you actually want the app to listen to an instrument.

This guide covers basic microphone checks for the current Android release. It does not establish a hardware fault, diagnose instrument tuning, or promise compatibility with a USB or Bluetooth MIDI accessory. Instructions for a different piano app may describe connections or settings that Melody Garden does not offer.

Check the permission without resetting the app

  1. Open Android Settings, find Apps, and select Melody Garden. The menu names can differ between phone manufacturers.
  2. Open Permissions and look for Microphone. If you want microphone practice, choose an available option that permits access while using the app.
  3. Return to Melody Garden and start the microphone activity again. If Android asks for permission each time, respond to that prompt for the current session.
  4. If you do not want to grant access, return to on-screen practice instead. You do not need to delete data or reinstall just to use the touch-based option.

Android permission settings control whether an app may access the microphone; changing them does not improve the quality of sound in the room. Treat permission and sound conditions as separate checks.

Try a clear, repeatable sound test

  1. Move away from a television, conversation, or loud background music. Place the phone so its microphone is not covered by your hand or resting against fabric.
  2. Play a single clear note and let it settle before trying the next one. Use the same short exercise for each check so that you change one thing at a time.
  3. If the instrument is quiet, try a more suitable phone position without placing it where it could fall or interfere with your playing.
  4. Compare the result after each small change. If feedback remains unreliable, continue on screen and collect details for support instead of repeatedly resetting progress.

These are practical troubleshooting suggestions, not a claim that every missed note is caused by noise. Input quality, the selected activity, and the installed release can all matter. A successful check on one phone does not establish identical behavior on another.

What to include in a support request

Describe your phone model, Android version, app version if visible, instrument, selected practice activity, and what feedback you expected. Say whether microphone permission is allowed and whether a quieter setting changed the behavior. A short description of the steps is more useful than simply saying that the app cannot hear you.

The current app analyzes microphone sound live on the device. It does not record, save, or upload microphone audio. Lesson results and settings are local; the developer does not automatically receive diagnostic reports or a copy of your practice. Contact support through the app page and avoid including unnecessary personal information.

Questions and answers

Can I keep learning if microphone access is denied?

Yes. Use on-screen practice. Microphone access is optional for microphone practice and the guitar tuner.

Should I reset progress to fix note detection?

Reset progress removes local learning information. It is not needed for the permission and room-noise checks in this guide.

Does the app upload my instrument recording?

The current release does not record, save, or upload microphone audio; it analyzes sound live on the device.
