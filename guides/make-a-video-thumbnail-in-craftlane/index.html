Make a Video Thumbnail in Craftlane

By Wonderworld Labs · Published 2026-11-14

Open Craftlane and choose the thumbnail maker. Start with one clear image, add a short headline if it helps explain the video, then export a PNG. View the export at thumbnail size before uploading it.

Choose an image that shows what the video delivers

Start with a frame or image you have permission to use. For a tutorial, show the result someone is trying to make. If you are making a thumbnail for a video about captions, show a frame with a readable caption and room for a short headline.

Choose your destination before placing text. Find its current thumbnail requirements and make room around important details so a crop will leave them visible.

Build the thumbnail in Craftlane

  1. Open the thumbnail maker and select the image you want to use.
  2. Place the main subject where it stays easy to recognize at a glance.
  3. Add a few words only if they contribute something the image cannot show. Give the letters a clear background and keep them away from the edges.
  4. Export the PNG and open it separately. Check the crop, the spelling and the size before uploading.

Check the export at the size a viewer will see

Shrink the finished image until it is about the width of a small video card. If you have to zoom in to read the text, shorten it or increase the type. If the picture is hard to recognize, try a closer crop or an image with fewer competing details.

Compare it with the video title. The title can explain the task while the image shows the result. Keep the final image and your original file so you can make another version later.

Questions and answers

Can I export the thumbnail as a PNG?

Yes. Craftlane supports PNG for thumbnail exports. Open the exported file to check how it will appear.

What if my thumbnail looks crowded on a phone?

Start by removing text that repeats the title. Then check the remaining words and the main image at small size.

Where does Craftlane process my selected image?

Craftlane processes selected media on your device. Its privacy page explains the separate website and advertising practices.
