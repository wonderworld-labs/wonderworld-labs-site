Linkloom: Solving Phrase Pyramids and Arithmetic Connection Chains

By Wonderworld Labs · Published 2026-11-14

Solve the phrase pyramid from the apex down by checking both left and right compound combinations for each parent tile, and use the 'Check all links' feature to isolate correct branches before swapping uncertain pieces.

Evaluate parent-child dual connections

In a phrase pyramid, each node must form a legitimate compound word or common phrase with both of its children below it. For example, SIDE above GREEN and BERRY must validly satisfy both 'SIDE-GREEN' or 'SIDE-BERRY' connections.

Swap tiles methodically across tiers

Tap any two tiles to swap their positions, or drag one tile directly onto another. If a tile clearly belongs in a compound phrase with two bottom nodes (like FIRE, WOOD, BACK), anchor that tile in tier 2 first.

Conserve check opportunities

You are allotted a limited number of checks per puzzle (typically 4). Arrange all six tiles into a plausible candidate pyramid before pressing 'Check all links' to maximize the feedback gained per check.

Questions and answers

Can I switch between Phrase Pyramids and Number Lab?

Yes, the top mode pills allow you to toggle seamlessly between Phrases, Numbers arithmetic pyramids, and word Fragments at any time.
