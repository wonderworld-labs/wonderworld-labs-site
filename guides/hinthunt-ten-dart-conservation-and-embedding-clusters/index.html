HintHunt: 10-Dart Management and Semantic Cluster Analysis

By Wonderworld Labs · Published 2026-11-14

Start with four conceptually distinct anchor words (such as an animal, a tool, a location, and an abstract emotion) to discover which domain lights up warmer, then cast narrower synonyms to hit the bullseye within 10 darts.

Cast four broad cardinal darts first

Don't guess randomly on turn 1. Fire four diverse probes across nature, physical objects, human actions, and conceptual states. Orbit the guide bot will immediately indicate whether a guess is cold, warm, or hot.

Interpret the bullseye concentric rings

Each dart lands on a visual target ring representing cosine similarity calculated privately on-device using trained MiniLM semantic embeddings. Outer blue rings indicate distant concepts; central yellow rings indicate close semantic cousins.

Zoom in on warm clusters

If a word like 'ocean' or 'swimming' lands inside the middle ring, test related aquatic creatures, marine geography, or coastal nouns (like 'dolphin', 'coral', 'whale') to zero in on the exact bullseye word.

Questions and answers

Are guesses processed online on a cloud server?

No, HintHunt computes semantic embeddings entirely on your device using a lightweight client model, keeping your guesses completely private.
