Learn piano: start with an online keyboard or an Android app

By Wonderworld Labs · Published 2026-10-28

To start learning piano, find a group of two black keys and the white key immediately to its left: C. Practise a short C–D–E pattern slowly before adding more notes. An online piano offers a quick browser start; an offline Android app can help you keep a practice routine without a connection. Neither replaces the feel of a physical keyboard.

Choose the tool for your first session

If you want to play piano online without installing an app, choose a browser instrument that makes sound when you tap its keys. Check whether it labels musical notes, which computer keys it maps to notes, and whether it works on your phone. A computer-keyboard letter is an input shortcut and may not be the musical note being played.

If you want lessons and practice available without a connection, Melody Garden provides piano and guitar learning on Android, including on-screen practice and local progress. Find C, then play a small pattern

  1. Find a group of two black keys and the C to its left.
  2. Play C, then D, then E, with one note per count.
  3. Return through D to C, keeping the gaps between notes even.
  4. Repeat the pattern twice before trying it from another C.

Black keys repeat in groups of two and three. The white key just before a group of two is C. Moving right from C, the next white keys are D and E. Use that repeating shape to find another C elsewhere on a keyboard.

Try C–D–E–D–C as a simple exercise, not as a song supplied by the app. Play one note for each steady count. If a note sounds late or wrong, slow down and repeat just the transition that caused trouble.

Move from a pattern to a song

Choose a short melody from a lawful source with notes you can identify. Work on one phrase at a time and separate finding the notes from keeping time. Start slowly enough that you can repeat the phrase without guessing; then join two phrases. This is a learning approach, not a claim about a particular song catalogue in Melody Garden.

On a physical piano, hand position, finger movement, key resistance, and pedals add skills that a touchscreen cannot reproduce. Use a virtual keyboard to explore notes, then practise those movements on an instrument when available.

Keep the next practice session simple

Finish by noting which pattern needs another attempt. The app keeps results and preferences locally, so avoid resetting progress or uninstalling if you want to retain them. Check current download access on the app page before planning around a new installation.

If an online piano makes no sound

  1. Tap the on-screen keyboard directly before trying computer-keyboard shortcuts. If tapping works, check the player’s shortcut labels and whether a text field has keyboard focus.
  2. Check media volume, the player’s volume control, and whether the browser tab or site is muted.
  3. Check where audio is playing: connected headphones or a Bluetooth speaker may be receiving it instead of the device speaker.
  4. If sound is still blocked, consult the player’s browser-audio instructions. Musicca’s help, for example, discusses autoplay settings; this is guidance for that service, not a Melody Garden web feature.

Before practising notes in a browser, check that one tapped key produces sound. A silent keyboard is a playback problem to resolve before judging your timing or note choices. Browser and instrument controls differ; use the help for the particular player you opened.

Questions and answers

Can I learn piano without owning a piano?

You can begin learning note locations and simple patterns on a virtual keyboard. Physical playing technique still needs practice on an instrument.

Can I play piano in my browser on this website?

A Wonderworld Labs web version is planned. This page is a beginner guide, not a playable keyboard. The current Melody Garden product is an Android app.

Does Melody Garden also help me learn guitar?

Yes, the current Android app supports guitar learning and an optional microphone-based tuner. Guitar fingering and string technique require a physical guitar.
