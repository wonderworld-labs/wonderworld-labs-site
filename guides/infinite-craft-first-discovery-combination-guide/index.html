Infinite Craft: Strategies for Reaching First Discoveries

By Wonderworld Labs · Published 2026-11-14

To unlock First Discoveries in Infinite Craft, branch beyond common cultural references into deep compound concepts, rare historical figures, specialized scientific terminology, and multi-word semantic hybrids.

  • Branch away from popular base elements
  • Pair high-tier modifiers with narrow subjects
  • Organize your active canvas by category

Branch away from popular base elements

Starting elements like Water, Fire, Earth, and Wind quickly converge on universal concepts like Steam, Mud, and Planet. To discover uncrafted items, chain specialized nouns with abstract descriptors, geological eras, or composite mythology.

Pair high-tier modifiers with narrow subjects

Once you craft rare elements such as Cyberpunk, Quantum, Renaissance, or specific country names, combine them with niche professions, mythical creatures, or architectural styles to generate unexplored recipe branches.

Organize your active canvas by category

Keep your workspace tidy by grouping candidate items along the left border. Testing pairings methodically against a dedicated modifier prevents redundant combinations and accelerates your path to a First Discovery badge.

Questions and answers

What qualifies as a First Discovery?

A First Discovery occurs when you are the first player globally to combine two elements into a completely new recipe result, permanently recording your discovery.
