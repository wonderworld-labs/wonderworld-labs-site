By Wonderworld Labs · Published 2026-11-14

Calibrate your mental ruler using standard real-world benchmarks—such as a 28-meter basketball court, a 3-meter ceiling height, or an adult human height—then scale the test subject proportionally using the responsive slider or pinch controls.

Establish known anchor dimensions

Every puzzle pairs an unfamiliar subject with a verified reference object. Memorize key baselines: a standard basketball court is 28 meters long, an Olympic swimming pool is 50 meters, and a residential story is about 3 meters tall.

Use visual subdivision on the grid stage

Mentally slice the larger object into multiples of the reference object. For instance, if a rocky cliff appears roughly two and a half times wider than the adjacent basketball stadium, adjust your ratio slider toward 2.4x to 2.5x.

Refine with micro-adjustment buttons

Use the broad slider for coarse sizing, then tap the + Grow and - Shrink stepper buttons for precision calibration before locking in your estimate.

Questions and answers

How are points awarded in Scale Safari?

Points are calculated on an exponential accuracy curve based on how close your estimated scale ratio is to the actual measured physical dimension, with perfect estimates earning 100 points.
