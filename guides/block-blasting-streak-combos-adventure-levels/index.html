Block Blasting Combo Guide: Streaks, Board Space, and Adventure Puzzles

By Wonderworld Labs · Published 2026-11-12

Consistent high scores in Block Blasting depend on maintaining multi-turn streaks by clearing at least one line per move, keeping a 3x3 pocket open for awkward shapes, and sequencing gem collections before the board fills.

Maintain multi-turn streaks for exponential multipliers

  1. Check all three piece options in your lower tray before placing any single block.
  2. Plan at least one row or column clear on every placement to preserve your active streak counter.
  3. Combine simultaneous horizontal and vertical clears on turn 3+ of a streak for massive bonus points.

In Block Blasting, single line clears grant modest points, but continuous streaks multiply score gains significantly. Breaking a streak resets your multiplier back to base.

Board preservation: protecting a 3x3 pocket

The most common cause of sudden game overs is receiving the 3x3 square block or 5-block L-shapes when no contiguous open space remains.

Always leave one central quadrant or lower corner cleared to accommodate large shapes without panic.

Adventure mode strategy: prioritizing gem tiles

In Adventure levels, the objective is collecting target gems within a limited number of moves rather than simply surviving. Clear lines containing gems even if it yields fewer raw points.

Questions and answers

How is the streak multiplier calculated?

Each consecutive placement that completes at least one line advances your streak tier, multiplying line-clear points by up to 5x.

What should I do when I receive three large awkward shapes?

Look for placements that immediately complete a line and consume part of the newly placed piece, creating breathing room.

Do Adventure level gems carry over across attempts?

No, each Adventure attempt requires reaching the target gem quota within that specific game session.
