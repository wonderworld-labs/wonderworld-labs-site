The Password Game: Managing Rule Sequencing and Roman Numerals

By Wonderworld Labs · Published 2026-11-14

Balance the Roman numeral sum early using subtractive notation like IV or IX, protect Paul's food count continuously, and isolate digits in your password to avoid accidentally triggering Roman letter evaluation.

Keep Roman numeral sums exact

Rule 9 requires Roman numerals to multiply or add to an exact total (such as 35). Remember that capital letters like V, X, L, C, D, and M are automatically parsed as Roman numerals. Choose lowercase characters elsewhere in your password to prevent unwanted sums.

Shield Paul the chicken and worm rations

Once Paul hatches, three caterpillar emojis must remain in the text input at all times. Do not edit near Paul's section quickly or paste over him, as any disruption to his food supply results in an instant game over.

Plan periodic atomic symbol placements

When adding two-letter atomic symbols whose atomic numbers sum to a target value, pick low-number elements like He (2) or Li (3) to fine-tune your total without disturbing existing sponsor names and captcha strings.

Questions and answers

How do I prevent new rules from breaking the digit sum?

When a new rule forces you to add digits (like a leap year or chess move), compensate immediately by lowering existing filler digits elsewhere in the string to keep the Rule 5 total constant.
