Find free perimeter arrows on first glance

Before tapping any central arrows, inspect the four borders of the puzzle board. Any arrow pointing directly off-screen with an open corridor can be launched immediately, widening maneuvering room for overlapping paths.

Trace reverse path dependencies

When an arrow appears trapped behind multiple perpendicular lines, trace its trajectory forward to see which specific arrow blocks its nose. Solve that blocker first, establishing a clear reverse dependency chain.

Avoid hasty taps to protect streak milestones

Arrow collisions reset your clean-solve bonus. Take a deliberate 5-second pause to verify the entire escape corridor before releasing an arrow, especially on high-density daily challenge boards.

How often do new daily challenge boards refresh?

A fresh daily challenge board unlocks every 24 hours at midnight local device time, complete with milestone tracking and streak counters.
