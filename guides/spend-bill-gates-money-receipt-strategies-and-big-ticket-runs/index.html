Spend Bill Gates’ Money: High-Value Portfolios and Fast Purchasing Runs

By Wonderworld Labs · Published 2026-11-14

To spend $100 billion efficiently, prioritize multi-billion dollar mega-assets like NBA franchises, cruise ships, and luxury skyscrapers, then fine-tune your final balance with everyday consumer items to reach a perfect zero balance.

Target the largest capital outlays first

Even buying 1,000,000 Big Macs barely dents a $100,000,000,000 fortune. To make rapid progress, buy high-ticket assets first: NFL teams ($3B each), Super Bowls ($5M each), and modern aircraft carriers ($10B each).

Build a themed ultra-luxury portfolio

Challenge yourself with realistic high-net-worth scenarios: buy 50 private islands, endow 20 university hospitals, fund 10 space rockets, and furnish 100 beachfront mansions with supercars and fine art.

Execute precision receipt trimming to reach $0

Once your remaining bank balance drops below $10,000, use smaller items like iPhones, gaming PCs, and flip-flops to exhaust every single dollar and trigger the 100% spent completion receipt.

Questions and answers

Can you sell assets back to recover cash?

Yes, the Sell button lets you liquidate any purchased item instantly at full value to rebalance your portfolio and experiment with different spending allocations.
