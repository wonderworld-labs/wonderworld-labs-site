Absurd Trolley Problems: Compare Your Reasoning Across Rounds

By Wonderworld Labs · Published 2026-11-14

After a round, write down your choice and the detail that mattered most. In the next round, do the same before looking back at the first answer. Compare the details and decide what changed your reasoning.

Write down the choice in one sentence

Summarize the two outcomes on the screen, then record the one you chose. Use the specific situation in that round so you can explain the decision later.

Name the detail that tipped your decision

You might focus on the number of people, whether someone took an action, or how certain an outcome seemed in the story. Pick the one detail you relied on most and describe it briefly.

Discuss one detail you would change

With a friend, compare notes after you each make a choice. Ask what single change to the fictional setup might change the answer. A shared choice can come from different reasons, and those reasons give you something concrete to discuss.

Questions and answers

What if I make a different choice in the next round?

Compare the two situations and identify the detail that changed. Use that detail to explain the difference between your answers.
