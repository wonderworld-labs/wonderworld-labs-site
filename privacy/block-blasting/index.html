Block Blasting Privacy Policy

Provider: Wonderworld Labs
Android app: com.blockblasting.puzzle
Effective 27 October 2026

About this game

Block Blasting is provided by Wonderworld Labs. This policy covers the current offline Android game, including Classic and Adventure play. The game has no account, internet permission, advertising, purchases, analytics, or network service. Gameplay information is not transmitted to us.

Information stored on your device

The game saves rounds, board and piece state, scores and personal bests, Adventure progress and rewards, collection badges, daily streak information, guide state, and preferences in local app storage. Preferences include sound, music, haptics, reduced motion, and placement hints. A previous valid save may be retained locally to recover from a damaged save. These files are not a developer-hosted cloud backup.

Permissions

The game uses Android’s vibration permission for optional haptic feedback. You can change haptics in Settings. It does not request camera, microphone, location, or contact permissions.

Retention and deletion

Progress and settings stay in app storage while you use the game. Clearing the app’s storage through Android settings or uninstalling removes local progress and saves. Automatic Android app backup is disabled. We do not hold a server copy and cannot restore progress after it is removed.

Sharing, advertising, and children

The current game does not send gameplay data or identifiers to us or advertisers and contains no advertising or analytics SDK. It does not collect a player’s age. If children use the game, their local gameplay progress is handled in the same way described above. Google Play and your device platform may separately process installation and update information under their own policies.

Contact and policy changes

For privacy questions, email contact@wonderworldlabs.com. If you contact us, we receive the email address and information you choose to send and use them to respond. Do not send private files, unlock secrets, or recordings unless necessary for your request. We will update this policy when the app’s data practices change.

Visiting this policy page

This policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information to serve and protect the website under its own privacy statement. We have not added analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts to this page. Website hosting is separate from local app processing.

See GitHub’s privacy statement.
