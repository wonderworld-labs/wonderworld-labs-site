Melody Garden Privacy Policy

Provider: Wonderworld Labs
Android app: com.melodygarden.melody_garden
Effective 27 September 2026 · Page updated 17 October 2026

This policy applies to Melody Garden’s current ad-free Android release. Other Wonderworld Labs apps and games have their own policies.

About this app

Melody Garden is provided by Wonderworld Labs. Melody Garden is an offline piano and guitar learning app for children, teens, and adults. This policy describes the current ad-free Android version. There is no account or developer-operated server.

Information saved on your device

The app saves your selected learning age range, instrument, settings, lesson progress, practice results, and favorites in local app storage. We do not receive this information or use it to identify you. You can clear it with Reset progress in Settings or by uninstalling the app.

Microphone

If you choose microphone practice or the guitar tuner, the app asks for microphone permission and analyzes sound live on your device to provide pitch feedback. The app does not record, save, or upload microphone audio. You can deny microphone permission and continue with on-screen practice.

Children and advertising

This version does not display advertising or include an advertising SDK. It does not send a child's age range, practice activity, or microphone audio to us. The age range is used on the device to adapt lessons.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell or share app usage information with advertisers or other third parties. Local progress remains until you reset it or remove the app. Google Play may process information about downloading and updating the app under Google's own policies.

Changes and contact

If we add online song downloads or advertising, we will update this policy and the app's disclosures before those features are released. For privacy questions, contact us at the email below.

Visiting this policy page

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information when serving this page, as described in its privacy statement. We have not added analytics, advertising or tracking scripts to this page. Website hosting is separate from the offline Android app.
