Arrow unleashed Privacy Policy

Effective 19 October 2026 · Page updated 19 October 2026

This policy describes Arrow unleashed's current offline Android release. Other WonderWorld Labs apps and games have their own policies.

About this app

Arrow unleashed is an offline Android puzzle game provided by WonderWorld Labs. This policy applies to the app with package ID com.wonderworldlabs.arrowunleashed. This release has no accounts, developer-operated game server, advertising, in-app purchases, analytics SDKs or other network services. The app does not request internet permission.

Information saved on your device

The app saves puzzle progress, remaining lives, hints, settings, daily puzzle completions, completion dates, XP, badges and daily goal progress in private local app storage. These records let you continue playing and keep your preferences and milestones. The app does not send this information to WonderWorld Labs or to third-party services.

Permissions and device features

The app uses Android vibration permission for optional feedback. You can turn vibration off in the app settings. It does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts or advertising identifier. The app is configured not to participate in Android app-data backup.

Sharing, retention and deletion

We do not receive, sell or share the app's locally stored gameplay information. Local records remain on your device until Android removes that app storage. To remove them, use Android Settings to clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. This release has no in-app reset-all control; restarting one puzzle does not delete all progress, settings or milestones. There is no developer account or server copy of your gameplay data for us to delete. Google Play and Android may separately process information about downloads, updates or platform diagnostics under their own policies.

Children and advertising

The same offline data practices apply to all players. The app does not ask for a name, email address, age or other personal profile, does not upload children's gameplay information, and contains no advertising or advertising SDK. A parent or guardian can remove local data using Android's storage controls or by uninstalling the app.

Support, changes and contact

For privacy or support questions, email contact@wonderworldlabs.com. If you choose to email us, your email service delivers your email address and the information you include so that we can respond. This is separate from the offline app; the app does not automatically send support messages, gameplay records or diagnostic reports. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information. If future releases change these data practices, we will update this policy and the app's disclosures.

Visiting this policy website

The policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information when serving website pages, as described in its privacy statement: GitHub privacy statement. We have not added analytics, advertising or tracking scripts to this policy page. Website hosting and any email you choose to send are separate from the offline Android app. Reading a copy of this policy inside the app does not itself make a website request.
